EDDMapS: lessons learned and retained files
Consolidated November 17, 2026

PURPOSE AND SCOPE
EDDMapS can supply public Minnesota occurrence records by code for recurring
imports. The app downloader is Rcode/Scripts/updateEDDMapS.R; its cleaned spatial
output is inputs/Dynamic/EDDMapS-current.rds. Neither is part of this cleanup.
This note replaces the exploratory assessment exports, page caches, and audits.

Historical counts below describe the November 17 field assessment of the OLD
128-taxon list (123 mapped taxa, 165 query IDs), not the current v2 list.
The November 12 assessment was slightly smaller; do not mix the two snapshots.
The current v2 mapping covers 107 of 116 taxa through 141 query IDs. Nine remain
unmatched. Mapping coverage is not evidence of Minnesota occurrence coverage.

WHAT TO KEEP USING
- reference/EDDMapS-taxon-queries.csv: downloader input, one ID per row.
  common is the app label; canonical_common preserves the source common name;
  scientific preserves the source scientific name.
- reference/EDDMapS-taxon-crosswalk.csv: one row per canonical taxon, including
  unresolved taxa and explanations of mapping decisions.
- reference/EDDMapS-taxon-overrides.csv: reviewed synonyms and scope exceptions.
- reference/EDDMapS-taxon-crosswalk-notes.txt: taxonomy details and API cautions.
- scripts/matchEDDMapSTaxa.R: regenerate mappings from the v2 canonical list.
  Run from the project root; requires jsonlite. --refresh refetches taxonomy.
- cache/EDDMapS-taxonomy-search.rds: reusable taxonomy responses and retrieval
  metadata. This avoids repeating remote searches when rebuilding mappings.
- cache/EDDMapS-taxonomy-candidates.csv: compact, inspectable candidate evidence;
  regenerated by the matching script.
- scripts/testIntegrationRules.R: local NAS history and EDDMapS mapping checks.
  Requires dplyr/sf and the current app inputs; it does not download occurrences.
The shared naming function is Rcode/Scripts/normalizeInvasiveCommonNames.R.

API PRACTICALITIES
Base: https://api.bugwoodcloud.org/v2
Documentation: https://developers.bugwood.org/
Example: /occurrence?state=27&subjectid=3055&pagesize=1000&page=1
Query each nonmissing numeric subject ID separately. Never submit blank/NA IDs:
the species restriction may be ignored. Iterate directly over IDs, rather than
using each ID as an index into the ID vector. Start at page 1 and continue until
the RAW response has fewer than 1000 rows; downstream filtering must not decide
whether another API page exists. Empty responses need their own handling.
The API delivered at most 2000 rows despite documentation describing 3000;
1000-row pages were verified. It returns more columns than the app requires.

state=27 is only a prefilter: it also returned Tabasco, Mexico, and Canada.
Require Minnesota AND United States geography, plus valid coordinates/year.
FIPS prefixes alone also collide internationally. Blank location labels need
individual-record country/state resolution if they are to be retained.
Some coordinates are centroids and some have substantial uncertainty; numeric
coordinates do not guarantee correct waterbody assignment. A bounding box is
only another screen, not an exact state polygon or a lake-location validation.

Parent taxon queries can return subspecies IDs. Synonym/parent queries can overlap;
deduplicate by objectid before discarding source identifiers. Respect the reviewed
crosswalk rather than accepting every descendant or a generic related taxon.
Watermilfoil and cattail hybrids intentionally share canonical app groups. Common
carp includes subspecies 57383 as well as parent 15300. Nonnative Phragmites uses
the introduced subspecies and its synonym, not the ambiguous generic species.

HISTORICAL SCALE AND TIMING
The November 17 pull made 202 page requests: 47,578 distinct downloaded records,
41 excluded as foreign, leaving 47,537 Minnesota records in 54 taxon groups.
46,394 had coordinates and a usable observation year. Of these, 43,074 were
Positive/Treated in 53 groups (41,407 Positive; 1,667 Treated); 2,356 were Negative
and 964 Eradicated. Missing coordinates affected 1,071 records; missing/invalid
years affected 749, with overlap. Exact taxon/coordinate/year deduplication left
42,629 Positive/Treated rows. These are occurrence counts, not infested lakes.
The page log spans 15:23:12 to 15:26:39 UTC, about 3.5 minutes for page retrieval
in that run. This excludes other processing and is not a runtime guarantee.
Responses totaled about 107 MB before trimming. No current-v2 occurrence count
or record-level EDDMapS-versus-NAS overlap estimate follows from these totals.

FIELD FINDINGS ACROSS TAXA (47,537 MINNESOTA RECORDS)
- infestationstatus was filled in every record: Positive, Treated, Negative,
  Eradicated. eradicationstatus matched it exactly, including missingness.
  Keep infestationstatus; the alias adds no information in this snapshot.
- reviewed was filled in 99.94%: 47,294 TRUE, 215 FALSE, 28 missing.
  This is unusually complete and supports an explicit review screen.
- identificationcredibility was filled in 35.17%; verificationmethod in 32.66%.
  Blank values are inconclusive, not proof of validity or invalidity. Requiring
  positive verification text would discard much of the usable presence data:
  only 9,185 of 43,074 usable presence candidates were Verified/Credible; 12,970
  had any verificationmethod. Reviewed TRUE does not guarantee no contradiction.
- populationstatus had only four values, all established, in one taxon.
  eradicationdate was entirely empty. Neither provides a general solution.
- observationdate was filled in 98.42%. Its year is the available detection-year
  proxy, not a demonstrated establishment date. yearaccuracy was only 3.60%
  filled and even mixed year numbers with precision labels.
- abundance was 45.24% filled and used five categories: Dense Monoculture,
  Dominant cover, Scattered Dense Patches, Scattered Plants, Single Plant.
  It was more standardized than the milfoil example suggested, but incomplete
  and not a general establishment test. A single observation need not reproduce.
- infestedarea was 57.22% filled, with multiple units/capitalizations. Positive
  area also occurred on 1,458 Negative/Eradicated records: area cannot override
  an absence status or independently establish current presence.
- recordbasis was 26.23% filled: 12,466 Human Observation and one Preserved
  Specimen. It describes evidence type, not population establishment.
- managementstatus was 92.97% filled, but 40,109 entries were Unknown; only
  4,086 carried another value. Explicit Not Found/Possibly Eradicated values
  merit attention; mere population of this column adds little confidence.

EXPLICIT COUNTEREXAMPLES TO USING INFESTATIONSTATUS ALONE
Among usable Positive/Treated records:
- 159 had reviewed FALSE, spanning 14 taxon groups.
- None had credibility Negative, but one had Incorrect (zebra mussel,
  objectid 4751973) and one had Unable to Confirm (rusty crayfish, 7625716).
  Both were reviewed TRUE and had Photographs as verificationmethod.
- Eight had managementstatus Not Found: Callinina georgiana (2),
  Cipangopaludina chinensis (3), Dreissena polymorpha (2), Faxonius rusticus (1).
- 4,395 had credibility Possible. Treating this as exclusion is a policy choice,
  not equivalent to finding an explicit Incorrect/Unable to Confirm flag.
Among usable absence records, 187 had credibility Negative, supporting its use
as an absence-related label here rather than a generic dubious-submission score.
798 Eradicated records said Possibly Eradicated in managementstatus: Phragmites
(768), Lythrum salicaria (26), Frangula alnus (4). Those labels differ in certainty;
the fields alone do not establish which interpretation is authoritative.
Example source records: https://www.eddmaps.org/distribution/point.cfm?id=4751973
and https://www.eddmaps.org/distribution/point.cfm?id=7625716 .
These are snapshot findings, not guarantees about future API submissions.

APP POLICY VERSUS WHAT THE DATABASE PROVES
The current downloader excludes explicit FALSE review and credibility Negative,
Incorrect, or Unable to Confirm; blanks/NA remain inconclusive. This is a chosen
screen, not an EDDMapS guarantee that all remaining records are established.
Treated alone is not eradication. The app treats infestationstatus Negative or
Eradicated, or managementstatus Not Found or Possibly Eradicated, as clearance
candidates. A lake/taxon/year clears only when all retained observations that
year are clearance candidates. A positive wins a mixed year; after the last
clearance year, the next retained presence starts a new infestation history.
These are modeling decisions. A point-level negative does not itself prove
whole-waterbody eradication; lake matching and year aggregation impose that
interpretation. Missing evidence is not converted to an affirmative assessment.
NAS now applies the same positive-wins/restart principles; unknown-only NAS
histories remain inconclusive, and unknowns alone do not restart cleared histories.

WHAT THIS INVESTIGATION DID NOT ESTABLISH
It did not demonstrate complete bidirectional EDDMapS/NAS synchronization,
transfer restrictions, a synchronization timetable, or unique-record counts
between systems. Do not infer redundancy or independence from field coverage.
It also did not directly validate established populations, eradication success,
or the spatial accuracy of every record. Retain provenance when refreshing.
