EDDMapS Minnesota record assessment - November 12, 2026

Scope: revised 128-taxon source list; 123 taxa mapped to 165 query identifiers.
Species groups retain the source list's combined cattail and watermilfoil scope.
Five unmapped taxa could not be queried by species; no related taxa were substituted.

202 occurrence-page requests returned 47,564 distinct record IDs (no duplicate IDs).
41 foreign records were excluded: 40 Tabasco, Mexico, and 1 Canada. Numeric state
ID 27 and FIPS prefixes are reused internationally. Confirmed Minnesota labels
were required; a blank-label record was checked through its individual country/state.

47,523 Minnesota records cover 54 source taxon groups.
46,380 have valid numeric point coordinates and an observation year.
43,061 of those are positive or treated records across 53 source taxon groups:
41,397 positive; 1,664 treated. The other geolocated/year records comprise
2,356 negative and 963 eradicated records.
1,143 Minnesota records lack a required coordinate/year field: 1,071 lack
coordinates, 749 lack a usable year, with 677 missing both (categories overlap).
No records without point coordinates supplied alternative nonpoint geometry.

Collapsing exact source-taxon/latitude/longitude/year repetitions leaves
42,616 positive-or-treated rows. This is not a count of infested lakes.
No snapping, lake matching, NAS-overlap assessment, or reconciliation of later
eradication reports against earlier positive records has been performed.
Treated records remain presence candidates; treatment alone is not eradication.
Some coordinates are explicitly centroids, so field completeness is not a
guarantee of precision adequate for lake assignment.

The API caps larger requests at 2,000 despite documentation stating 3,000.
The complete count uses verified 1,000-record pages. For the four largest
queries an additional page beyond the final short page was checked and empty.
Completed responses total about 107 MB of JSON before discarding extra fields.

Files: taxon-summary.csv; counts.csv; status-summary.csv; page-log.csv;
pagination-checks.csv; excluded-geography.csv; record-audit.rds.
occurrences-positive-or-treated.csv contains latitude, longitude, species, state,
year. Its species names exactly match the canonical invasive-species CSV at
inputs/Static/invasives_list_revised_2026.csv, including the combined hybrid groups.
occurrences-all-statuses.csv retains the original species_code field instead.
observed-taxon-crosswalk.csv maps returned EDDMapS codes, including subspecies,
to the source scientific names. Counts are in taxon-summary.csv; descendant
ancestry is recorded in descendant-details.rds.

Reproduce: Rscript upstream/Admin/EDDMapS/scripts/assessEDDMapSRecords.R
Resume interrupted unchanged inputs: append --resume. Default starts a fresh snapshot.
